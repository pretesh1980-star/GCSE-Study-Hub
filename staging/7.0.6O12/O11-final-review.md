## Honest assessment and saved progress

Only unassisted, unseen structured responses set the existing `Cell Biology|Active transport · O11` section percentage. Whole-topic Cell Biology mastery remains separate and unchanged. Guided work and all four unmarked written marks contribute no automatic mastery. A 6/8 result is 75% of structured questions, never a claimed 12-mark total. Low-score feedback does not claim strong achievement.

Written-attempt screens reject obvious letters/placeholders and seek a contextual relationship, without a word-count quota. They are lightweight language heuristics, not scientific markers. Incorrect but meaningful reasoning can reach guided review; unusual valid wording may require rephrasing. The selected structured response is checked separately; the writing is always labelled unmarked. Optional originals and improvements remain available as reviewed drafts.

New wording uses assessment version 3, new question IDs and `nextFinalForm` inside the existing `activeO11` record. Versions 1 and 2 retain their original questions, diagrams, scores and writing. Pending old attempts resume; students may instead start a fresh final-version form while preserving earlier answers as unfinished practice history. Legacy fixtures were generated by the actual earlier software, with unrelated O10 and whole-topic evidence; these tests do not inspect a learner’s private storage.

Two unseen version-3 forms can earn independent structured evidence. After both forms have been seen, “Try again independently” still opens blank fields but explicitly labels the repeat as practice with no new mastery. Previous scores remain saved; historical answers, models and marking checklists stay hidden during a pending attempt. Submission is required before results appear. Duplicate submissions/post-result edits cannot add credit. An unfinished check cannot be abandoned through the retake button to erase assistance. Reload alone preserves independence; system teaching/help or declared outside help makes that attempt practice permanently.
