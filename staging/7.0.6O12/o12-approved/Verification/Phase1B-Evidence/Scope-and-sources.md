# Scope, sources and implementation addendum

18 October 2026. Static review materials only; no runtime or examination interface is supplied.

## Official authority and reused evidence

- Current AQA 8464 Biology subject content, Cell Biology 4.1, verified this review: https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/science/gcse/science-8464/specification/biology-subject-content
- Current AQA 8464 scheme of assessment and assessment objectives, verified this review: https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/science/gcse/science-8464/specification/scheme-of-assessment
- Phase 1 official source register: `../O12-phase1/O12-Official-Sources.md` in the project outputs (not redistributed inside this ZIP). Existing official specification, specimen and 2025 assessment research was reused. Copyrighted official papers are not included in this pack.

The catalogue records specification, AO, lesson and visual mappings for all 24 parts per tier. AO classification is our design judgement; it is not official AQA validation. The 16/16/8 allocation reflects 40/40/20 balance. No Cell Biology subsection is HT-only; Higher uses less scaffolding, more demanding calculations and evaluation. An unfamiliar cell is supplied with the information necessary for application under 4.1.1.3.

## Sampling limits

4.1.1.1-5, 4.1.2.1-3 and 4.1.3.1-3 are represented. Do not infer full bullet-by-bullet coverage from this: microscope slide preparation, drawing construction, microscopy history and relative area, some sperm/xylem/phloem adaptations, the full division-and-repair sequence, single-/multicellular transport comparisons, urea/ventilation examples, rate of water uptake and constructing graphs remain under-sampled. These are coverage limits, not absent lessons or changes to the curriculum.

RP01 appears in microscope procedure, observation records and magnification context; the six-mark group also includes resolution, so six marks must not be described as six marks of practical manipulation. RP02 has two calculation marks and six marks for interpretation/evaluation. Physical practical competence is not assessed by these papers.

## Visual and integrity dependencies

All required review assets now exist. F-CILIATED replaces the proposed familiar muscle example. F/H-MASS-TABLE are separate 7a pilot batches; 7b investigation graphs/tables cannot be used to read off the calculation answers. F/H-DRAWING intentionally omit calibration because this is the assessed defect; the omission is documented, not a missing production dependency. Cell labels A/B and their text alternatives describe geometry without giving the assessed organelle name.

All 22 assets were visually inspected at 320 pixels wide; 390-pixel images and scalable original SVGs are supplied. Question text versions include equivalent prose and semantic data tables. PDF/UA certification, assistive-technology testing and physical iPhone Safari verification remain unperformed.

Future interface development still needs its own approval and tests for navigation, restoration, tier isolation, answer submission, manual-review labels, duplicate credit and mastery integration. A review-paper score must not by itself award whole-topic Secure. These runtime requirements have not been implemented or tested in Phase 1B.

## Numerical and marking checks

- F2c: 24 mm / 600 = 0.04 mm = 40 micrometres.
- H2c: 72 mm / 24000 = 0.003 mm = 3 x 10^-6 m.
- F5b: surface area 24 mm²; volume 8 mm³; ratio 3:1 or equivalent 24:8. Method credit is limited when arithmetic is wrong.
- H5b: surface area 1.5 mm²; volume 0.125 mm³; ratio 12:1; four times Q's 3:1. Error-carried-forward comparison is capped at one of two marks.
- F7a: (2.64 - 2.40) / 2.40 x 100 = +10%.
- H7a: (2.92 - 3.20) / 3.20 x 100 = -8.75%, rounded to -8.8%.
- F7b zero mass change: about 0.40 mol/dm³. H7b: about 0.32 mol/dm³; justified graphical estimates 0.31-0.33 accepted. Values describe the sampled tissue/conditions, not every plant tissue.
- Six-mark schemes use best-fit levels 0 / 1-2 / 3-4 / 5-6. Human judgement remains necessary; model responses illustrate rather than prescribe exact wording.

The 861 logged conditions include per-file preservation assertions; they are not 861 independent educational or application tests. The reused application evidence is 1154/1154. No new live-exam, learner-pilot or real-device validation is claimed.
