# GCSE Study Hub — Permanent Educational Standards

“Learning should feel like discovery, not a school assignment.”

Apply across future subjects, topics and subsections when development is authorised. These standards do not authorise rewriting unrelated lessons. Use the existing Osmosis Lab as the benchmark, with topic-appropriate methods.

The experience should be engaging for teenagers, scientifically accurate, clear, visual, interactive, accessible and mobile-friendly. Prioritise genuine understanding over superficial gamification.

1. Spark curiosity before teaching definitions.
2. Show concepts visually wherever useful.
3. Use familiar analogies with accurate explanations and explicit limits.
4. Break information into small chunks.
5. Minimise unnecessary reading.
6. Include frequent meaningful interaction, approximately every 30–60 seconds where appropriate.
7. Turn mistakes into learning opportunities.
8. Provide progressive hints, useful feedback and opportunities to retry.
9. Require independent understanding before awarding mastery; watching and supported practice earn no mastery.
10. Preserve complete specification coverage.
11. Offer optional deeper explanations.
12. Explain why each topic matters.
13. Connect previous and subsequent learning.
14. Make learning enjoyable without superficial gamification.
15. Choose teaching methods appropriate to the topic.
16. Prioritise educational outcomes, accessibility and mobile usability.
17. Reuse successful components to reduce development costs.

## Working safeguards

Preserve existing questions, assessments, progress, course/board/tier distinctions and storage compatibility. Keep candidates separate from approved live builds. Add no paid dependencies. Test focused behaviour and regressions, report exact totals and distinguish desktop/responsive checks from real-device Safari testing. Publication and the next lesson require explicit approval.
