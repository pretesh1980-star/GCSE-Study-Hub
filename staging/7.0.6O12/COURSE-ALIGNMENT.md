# Science course alignment — provisional

**School qualification: unconfirmed.** The Study Hub's current AQA Biology pathway was originally built around separate GCSE Biology **8461**. AQA Combined Science: Trilogy **8464** shares seven broad Biology topics but excludes some separate-Biology content. AQA Combined Science: Synergy **8465** organises science differently. Do not use this app as the sole exam checklist until the school's entry code is known.

## Shared content added in Build 5.7

These seven lesson steps are present in both AQA Biology 8461 and AQA Combined Science: Trilogy 8464. Each has a short lesson, recall card, worked example, one quick question and one written explanation question.

| Topic | New step |
|---|---|
| Organisation | Cancer and risk factors |
| Organisation | Treating cardiovascular disease |
| Organisation | Leaf tissues and their jobs |
| Homeostasis and response | The endocrine system |
| Inheritance, variation and evolution | Inherited disorders and screening |
| Inheritance, variation and evolution | Sex chromosomes and inheritance |
| Ecology | Human impacts and biodiversity |

The app now has 67 AQA Biology lesson steps, each with an example and two step questions. Twelve label activities cover the seven topic groups. These numbers describe **app content**, not complete specification coverage.

## Still needed for an exam-ready course

- Confirm whether the school enters **8461**, **8464**, or **8465**, and which tier applies. Preserve the current saved progress when the course is aligned.
- Audit every assessable specification point against lessons, cards, questions and practical preparation. Mark coverage as complete, partial or missing, including Foundation/Higher restrictions and separate-only content.
- Continue checking examples for scientific accuracy and add more exam-style application practice. Topic mapping does not make this an official AQA course or mark scheme.
- Add substantial unfamiliar-context questions, data and graph interpretation, calculations, practical-method evaluation, and extended written responses with clear marking guidance.
- Use school teaching and supervised practical work alongside official AQA past papers and mark schemes. The app's self-assessed written responses are revision practice, not examiner marking.
