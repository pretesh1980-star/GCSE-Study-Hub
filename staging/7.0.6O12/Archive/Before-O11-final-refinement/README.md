## Architecture and extending packs

- `common-content.js`: extracted Build 4 topics, lessons, questions and tests.
- `curriculum.js`: board availability, qualification IDs, topic maps, shared-lesson references, lesson overrides, sources, question metadata and tier filtering.
- `biology.js`: expanded Biology lessons, practical preparation and questions for the supported pathways.
- `biology-cards.js`: original AQA Biology subsection cards and labelled visuals.
- `biology-practice.js`: Cell Biology worked examples, questions and quiz diagrams.
- `biology-extended-practice.js`: worked examples and two quick questions for every other AQA Biology lesson subsection.
- `biology-core-gaps.js`: seven shared-core lesson steps with cards, examples and questions.
- `biology-diagrams.js`: interactive Biology label activities.
- `maths.js`: AQA Maths lessons, expanded practice bank, worked examples, revision cards, videos and diagrams.
- `app.js`: migration/storage, selectors, rendering, sessions, marking and progress.
- `design.css`: site presentation and responsive layout.

Topic IDs are stable learning identifiers; a board may change topic titles/order/unit headings without losing records. Each question has a stable ID, subject, topic, type, model/accepted answers, marks, difficulty, tier and optional question style. Resolved questions capture board/specification/tier at session start. Add higher-only questions with `tier: 'higher'`; unsure and Foundation exclude them. Tier and board records are intentionally independent; common-core scores are retained, not automatically credited as board mastery.

To add a pack, register its supported qualification, define the topic-to-shared-content mapping, then add audited lesson overrides and original questions with references to the exact specification. Update coverage notes and regression tests. Do not rename stable IDs casually or reuse them for a different learning objective.
