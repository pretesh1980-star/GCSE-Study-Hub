## Curriculum wording and limits

Cached AQA 4.6.3.4 explicitly includes completing prescribed antibiotic courses. Teaching expresses adherence to the prescribed plan and any updated clinician instructions, without claiming that every course kills every bacterium or that mutations occur because bacteria need them. This is curriculum reasoning, not personal dosing/prescribing advice. Inactive-pathogen vaccination is the specification model, not a claim about the formulation of every vaccine. Drug-trial data are illustrative; improved and adverse-effect counts may overlap. Written answers use model-answer self-assessment. Coverage is internal evidence-based assessment against the cached specification, not external accreditation.
