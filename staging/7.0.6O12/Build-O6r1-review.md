# GCSE Study Hub 7.0.6O6r1 — simpler explanations

Unpublished revision of O6, responding to the four review screenshots. No O7, publication or GitHub push.

- Removed “O5” from teaching text. Explanations now stand alone for a first-time visitor.
- Gene slider now selects three example gene sections. Each selection links DNA instructions to a protein and a simple job: building cell parts, helping a reaction, or moving a substance. Positions are illustrative, not a real chromosome map.
- Play sequence now supplies slow device narration and matching captions. Growth → DNA copying before mitosis → matching copies moving apart → two cells. Play/pause, rewind and Sound on/off available. Reduced motion still uses learner-controlled frames; captions remain available.
- Mitosis subtitle now has its own line and cannot break into “Mitosi / s”.
- Stem cells are introduced as cells without a particular job. A short narrated illustrated story uses a trainee analogy, compares embryo/bone-marrow/meristem sources and explains differentiation with blood, nerve and root-hair examples. The analogy is bounded: real cells respond to signals, not conscious decisions.
- Shorter case questions and explanations; technical detail sits in optional notes. GCSE terms and the relevant risks/ethics remain.
