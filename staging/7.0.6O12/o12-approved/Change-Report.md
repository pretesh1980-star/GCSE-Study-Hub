## Exact changes

- Foundation 7b and Higher 7b: indicative marking guidance now explicitly credits comparing several plant tissue types under matched conditions, repeating within each type and comparing zero-change concentrations. The top band requires developed reasoning addressing generalisation. Repeats of one tissue improve reliability but cannot test the universal claim. Agreement among sampled tissues cannot prove it for every plant tissue.
- Both six-mark rubrics retain holistic best-fit levels, 0–6 marks, equivalent valid science and alternative approaches (including a justified counterexample). No mandatory wording or keyword checklist.
- Foundation model response now describes the tissue comparison and its implications. Higher model remains unchanged because it already calls for separate tissue tests; the expanded rubric supplies the matched-condition detail.
- Higher 5b now asks “how many times as large as Q’s it is”. Its answer remains 12:1 and four times Q.
