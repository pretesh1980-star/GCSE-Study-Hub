# Specialised Cells — curriculum mapping

11 October 2026. Scope: AQA Combined Science Trilogy (8464), Foundation and Higher. The new experience and its questions are enabled only where the existing Cell Biology journey applies. Other boards, Separate Biology, Synergy and unconfirmed courses retain their existing teaching and question filtering. No mapping is inferred for them.

The audit used the existing `spec-audit-data.js` 4.1.1.3 mapping, `biology-cell-completion.js`, the resolved Trilogy lessons, and [AQA's current Biology subject content](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/science/gcse/science-8464/specification/biology-subject-content). The canonical curriculum files and original questions are unchanged.

| Content | Existing mapping and treatment in O3 |
|---|---|
| Nerve, muscle and sperm cells | Core named animal examples, 4.1.1.3. Axon/branches, contraction/mitochondria, tail/energy are assessed on both tiers. |
| Root hair cells | Core plant example, 4.1.1.3. Large surface area; water by osmosis and mineral ions by active transport link to 4.1.3.2–3 and 4.2.3.2. |
| Xylem | Core, 4.1.1.3 and 4.2.3.2. Mature vessel elements are dead; a hollow continuous lumen carries water/mineral ions, and lignin strengthens walls. |
| Phloem | Core, 4.1.1.3 and 4.2.3.2. Living elongated cells carry dissolved sugars through end-wall pores. Detailed companion-cell structure and loading mechanism are enrichment: AQA says detailed phloem structure/mechanism are not required. Existing companion-cell teaching is preserved. |
| Egg cell / haploid nuclei | Related learning, Inheritance 4.6.1.1–2. Optional egg comparison and sperm-nucleus explanation; not independently required as another named 4.1.1.3 example. Human 23 + 23 → 46 is a simplified fertilisation illustration. |
| Acrosome | Clearly marked enrichment for this milestone. No independent-check credit depends on recalling this term. |
| Guard cells | Related learning, Organisation 4.2.3.1–2. Pair of cells around a stomatal pore; gas exchange/water-loss trade-off. Optional comparison here; the original Organisation teaching remains required in its own context. |
| Unfamiliar cells and comparisons | 4.1.1.3 application: infer the advantage of a supplied feature and explain structure → function. Independent checks include folds/surface area, energy demand, and animal/plant or xylem/phloem comparison. |
| Differentiation | Existing milestone 6 content remains intact. O3 does not move or rewrite that lesson. |

All new independent items have `tier: both`, board AQA, specification 8464, course Trilogy, section Specialised cells and section-only mastery. No Higher-only content has been relabelled as shared or added to other board pathways. Extension material is excluded from the O3 independent questions.

Model limits are stated beside each demonstration: neurone marker is a travelling electrical change rather than a particle; muscles pull and filaments slide; sperm bends its tail; root hair is a single-cell extension; vessel elements form xylem vessels; phloem direction depends on source/sink. Still-frame controls offer the same explanations without motion or audio. Models are schematic and not to scale.
