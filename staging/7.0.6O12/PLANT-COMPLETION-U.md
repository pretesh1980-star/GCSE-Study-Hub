## Limitations

Drawings, graph construction and extended answers use model-answer self-assessment. The existing leaf schematic is stylised, with a combined vein symbol and limited guard-cell detail; its limitations and both transport tissues are explicitly explained. Actual leaf/stomatal microscope observations and potometer work require classroom practical activity. Online coverage does not certify physical performance. Data are illustrative; transpiration factor responses are conditional, and uptake is not identical to water loss. Completion is an internal evidence-based judgement, not exam-board endorsement.
