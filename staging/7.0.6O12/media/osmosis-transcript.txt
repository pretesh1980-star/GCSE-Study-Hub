Osmosis — video transcript

0–10 seconds: Blue spheres represent water. Gold spheres represent dissolved sugar. Water is the solvent; sugar is a solute.
10–20 seconds: In this model, water can cross the membrane. Sugar cannot. Partially permeable means only some particles pass through.
20–30 seconds: Water crosses both ways, but more moves towards the concentrated side. Net movement is the difference between the opposing flows.
30–40 seconds: At equal concentrations, water keeps moving both ways at equal rates overall. There is no net movement. Particles have not stopped.
40–50 seconds: In dilute surroundings, water enters cells through their membranes. A plant wall resists expansion. An animal cell may swell and burst.
50–55 seconds: Put a cell in a more concentrated solution. Pause and predict: which way does water move?
55–60 seconds: Water leaves overall. The cell loses water and shrinks.

All particle sizes and rates are qualitative illustrations.
