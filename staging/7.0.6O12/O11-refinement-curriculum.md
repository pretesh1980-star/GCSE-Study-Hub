# O11 refinement — curriculum and tier check

15 October 2026. Reuses the prior verified [AQA Trilogy 8464 Biology specification](https://www.aqa.org.uk/subjects/science/gcse/science-8464/specification/biology-subject-content) and the preserved O11 coverage report. Scope: 4.1.3.3 Active transport, with comparison links to 4.1.3.1 diffusion and 4.1.3.2 osmosis. No claim of certification of full Cell Biology or unmarked writing.

These core requirements are shared by Foundation and Higher. The established board/course/tier contexts remain intact, with explicit tests of separate records. No Higher-only gate or extra molecular mechanism has been introduced. Water-potential terminology remains paired with the dilute/concentrated model; osmosis is net water movement through a partially permeable membrane. Membrane proteins are a concise explanatory scaffold; no ATP-cycle or co-transport detail is assessed. The gut drawing continues to describe an overall route through intestinal cells, not one direct gut-to-blood pump.

The bicycle analogy is explicitly limited: gradients are concentration differences, not physical slopes. Respiration supplies transport energy. New material and original marking checklists are labelled GCSE-style learning resources. The four written marks are available for human/self-review, not automatically awarded or included in mastery.
