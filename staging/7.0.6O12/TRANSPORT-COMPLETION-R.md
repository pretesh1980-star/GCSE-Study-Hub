## Practical and quantitative additions

RP02 remains AQA Trilogy required practical 2. Preparation uses consistent plant tissue and dimensions, safe teacher-supervised cutting, initial/final mass, a concentration range with stated units, controlled time/temperature/volume, consistent blotting, independent repeats, per-repeat percentages and means. Plot gains and losses, estimate zero crossing, investigate rather than automatically discard anomalies, improve repeat count/spacing/measurement precision. Length observations cannot replace required mass measurements. Safety addresses sharps, tasting, spills and hygiene.

Worked teaching dataset has zero crossing near 3 g/100 mL; separate assessment data have initial mass 2.50 g and final masses 3.00/2.80/2.60/2.40/2.20 g, giving +20/+12/+4/−4/−12% at 0/2/4/6/8 g per 100 mL. The plotted line crosses zero at 5, with an explicit graph-reading tolerance. A revealable graph supports paper plotting and interpretation without a new drawing interface. Numeric rate practice accepts equivalent g/min, mg/min and g/s values, rejecting mass-only, percentage, incompatible units and wrong magnitudes.
