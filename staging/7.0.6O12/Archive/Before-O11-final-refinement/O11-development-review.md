## The learning journey

| Stage, exact displayed order | Purpose |
|---|---|
| 1. A root’s puzzle | Establish why roots need to absorb mineral ions against a gradient; reveal the energy-powered solution. |
| 2. Move against the gradient | Compare stepped diffusion and active-transport models with respiration energy available or off. |
| 3. Roots and your gut | Connect mineral-ion and glucose absorption to real needs; distinguish root water uptake by osmosis. |
| 4. Compare the three processes | Compare substance, direction, membrane, energy and example; match three short scenarios with guided feedback. |
| 5. Apply and explain | Two short applications, progressive hints and feedback/model explanations; improve saved drafts. |
| 6. Show your understanding | Two fresh independent forms, each 10 structured items plus a written explanation, followed by Correct/Review results. |

All stages are freely available. Short Hollie guides reuse the existing device-speech helper at rate 0.8; the exact spoken text remains visible as a full transcript. Listen and Stop do not run an automatic visual timeline. The model advances only by a student’s button press. It illustrates one net transfer, not an observed rate or the full random motion of every molecule. Labels become normal-sized wrapping text on small screens. No new video or paid media service was added.
