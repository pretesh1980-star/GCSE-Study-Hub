A 60-second original computer-rendered educational animation, 960 × 540, 15 fps, H.264/yuv420p MP4 with fast-start metadata. Shaded particles and schematic cells illustrate the science; this is a diagram-style animation rather than a photorealistic 3D film. It has baked-in English captions and a separate caption track/transcript; no narration or music.

## Educational scope

Water versus sugar; selective membrane permeability; opposing water movement with a greater net flow towards the more concentrated solution; equal opposing flows at equilibrium; plant wall resistance and animal-cell swelling; prediction of water loss in concentrated surroundings. Quantities, particle sizes and rates are illustrative, not experimental measurements. The video does not dynamically reproduce concentrations reaching equilibrium; equal conditions are a separate scene.

A text description accompanies the player. Captions are always visible in the video; the optional track repeats them for accessibility. This video supplements the existing adjustable membrane model and tutor, without replacing the learner's prediction-and-explanation work. It makes no claim that viewing proves understanding.
